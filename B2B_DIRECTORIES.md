# B2B Directory Registration List

## Tier 1: High Authority Directories (Register First)
1. **Alibaba** - https://www.alibaba.com/
   - Free basic listing, premium options available
   - Highest traffic B2B marketplace for eyewear

2. **Made-in-China** - https://www.made-in-china.com/
   - Strong for manufacturing/sunglasses category
   - Free supplier account available

3. **Global Sources** - https://www.globalsources.com/
   - Premium B2B platform for electronics/eyewear
   - Free basic listing

4. **DHgate** - https://www.dhgate.com/
   - B2B/B2C marketplace, good for smaller orders
   - Free seller account

5. **eBay Wholesale** - https://www.ebay.com/b2b
   - B2B section of eBay, large buyer base
   - Free listing with commission on sale

## Tier 2: Industry-Specific Directories
6. **Eyewear Daily** - https://www.eyewear-daily.com/
   - Industry publication with directory
   - Free basic listing

7. **Optician.org** - https://www.optician.org/
   - Professional optician directory
   - Free supplier listing

8. **Sunglasses News** - https://www.sunglassesnews.com/
   - Industry news with business directory
   - Free basic listing

9. **Vision Monday** - https://www.visionmonday.com/
   - Eyewear industry publication
   - Free supplier profile

10. **The Eyewear Hub** - https://www.theeyewearhub.com/
    - B2B eyewear marketplace
    - Free registration

## Registration Strategy
1. Start with Tier 1 (highest traffic, most competitive)
2. Move to Tier 2 (industry-specific, targeted buyers)
3. Add Tier 3 (general B2B, broader reach)
4. Complete with Tier 4 (niche/regional, specialized)
5. Update profiles quarterly with new products and certifications
